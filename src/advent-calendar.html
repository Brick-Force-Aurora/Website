 Bricktastic X-Mas Calendar

How many days left until Christmas? We’ll help you count!

Open a door of our advent calendar each day and discover amazing presents. But remember, you can only open one door each day: door 7 opens on 7 December, door 8 on 8 December, and so on.

The calendar opens on December 1 - come back then!

A fan re-creation of the original calendar. The present behind each door is picked at random from the festive items of the old Brick-Force shop; no login is needed and nothing is added to your inventory.

 My Maps

Why Should I Create a Map?

Map building is one of the highlights of Brick-Force. It's your chance to let your creativity shine and play a part in the ever-evolving landscape of the Brick-World.

Once your map has been registered, other players will have the opportunity to rate it out of 10. Each player has 50 "Stardust" per day. Stardust is used to reward map creators based on your map rating. Map creators will receive the equivalent in Brick Points based on player ratings.

Please note: If a map you previously registered is inactive for more than 3 months (meaning if it hasn’t been edited, downloaded or played), we reserve the right to delete it. Now then, get building, soldier!

Have your maps rated by everyone who plays them and be rewarded with brick points.

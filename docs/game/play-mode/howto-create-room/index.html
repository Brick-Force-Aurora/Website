 How to create a room

Hi dearest player of Brick-Force! Let us get started by choosing a channel to play in:

  • Beginner is for brand new players who want to check out the game in a small tutorial first. Once you have reached the level of Corporal, you will no longer be able to enter the beginner channel.
  • Battle takes you directly into play mode. For the soldiers among you.
  • Map Edit will lead you right to the Build mode. For the born architect.
  • ClanMatch is for Clan fights only.

Double-click on any channel you want to join or click the “Play” symbol on the top left. The tabs sort all existing channels. They are meant to give you a better overview. Don’t worry, the decision you make here is not final. You can always change channels within the game.

Choose the beginners' channel if you need a game introduction, the editing channel to build maps or the battle channel to play against others. Channel selection is not final.

Here, you have the opportunity to either build a brand new map or to play against other people in the various play modes. Just press the “Create” button, and you will end up in the map creation interface if you have chosen a map editing channel or the play mode selection interface if you are in a battle channel.

Press “Create” to either create a new map or a room to play against others.

Now that we have chosen one of the battle channels, we can play in various play modes. Each of these modes has their own way of gameplay. Jump directly to the tutorial part for Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch, CTF or Defosion.

“Title” refers to the title of the room. There will be a default line in here whenever you play. You can change this line by clicking on the “Title” bar. A room title is mandatory.

The little lock symbol on the top right of your game window enables you to encode your game with a password. Whenever somebody wants join in on the game, they will have to enter this password. Right now, a password can consist of 4 characters. Creating a password is optional.

With the “Max Players” option, you can define how many players you intend to allow to play with you. The maximum number you can select here is 16, including yourself.

Of course, you can choose the map you want to play, too. Anybody who has built a map will be able to register it, and they show up right here.

Name the room, decide how many people you want to play with and select a map.

This interface is the point where you pick the game mode that you want to play in. Jump directly to the tutorial part for Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch, CTF or Defusion.

Some modes require a time limit which is alterable.

“Goal” is the amount of players that need to be defeated in order to end the time limit abruptly. This means that even if the time is not up yet, if 50 players have been defeated by one team, the round ends, and that team wins.

“Weapon Options” lets you pick if either all weapons are allowed in your game or you want everyone to only use pistols for example.

“Mid-Game” lets people join while the game is already running; uncheck if you don’t want this.

Check “Team Balance” when you want a balanced game. For example, if you start a game 1 vs. 1 and check “Mid-Game”, everyone who joins could join your opponent’s team so you might end up playing 1 vs. 8. If checked, “Team Balance” will automatically sort players into teams.

Choose a game mode and press “OK” to get to the next screen.

For our example, we have chosen Team Deathmatch. Here, you can see the room name and the two teams: red and blue. Anyone who first started a game will be labeled the “Master”. The Master is the only one who sees a “Start” button, all other players who want to join will see the button “Ready”. Only when all joined players have pressed “Ready”, the game will start.

“Team change” lets you swap team colors.

In the lower left corner of your screen, you will see the map you have chosen. The thumbnail represents the map itself. Right next to it, there is a summary of which options you chose for this map.

Clicking on the small cog symbol next to the map name will bring you back one screen to change options if needed.

We now have a multitude of tools and items which will make the action-packed life of a Brick soldier that little bit easier. Enjoy utilizing the new auto reload function, ammo top-ups and super speedy boots in the various shooter modes. The new Medipack will heal your wounds and the Heartbeat Radar will help you find hidden enemies.

Press “Start” to begin the game and change your Team color as you like.

We are in the middle of a game now! This is the interface you see right after entering in Team Deathmatch.

In the top left corner, there is your radar (you will see your own team only) and an alarm clock symbol. The latter shows you how much time has passed in this round.

The top shows a red, a blue and a grey field. The numbers in red and blue indicate how many members of one team have been defeated by the other. “50” indicates the aforementioned “Goal” option.

The green crosshair is your aiming help. If you have a Sniper Rifle in your hands, you will need to right-click to zoom in and see a more exact crosshair. Left-click will shoot.

The bottom left bar shows your health status, the one on the bottom right shows your current ammo (30) and which kind of gun you are using. Press “R” to reload from your ammo storage (150).

Shoot with left-click, watch the countdown in the top left and reload by pressing “R”. Swap between weapon types by pressing 1, 2, 3 or 4.

Pressing “Esc” will take you to this interface. Exit the room, quit the game (if you have to) or choose “Cancel” if you want to leave the menu. This is the best way to leave your game window, too.

Hint: Pressing “Esc” will NOT stop the time or make you invisible so watch out for opponents!

Press “Esc” to view this menu.

Pressing and holding down “Tab” will bring up this interface. All players, including Ranks and names will be listed here. You will also be able to check how many other players you have defeated (Kills) or wounded (Assists) and how often you have died (Deaths). “Score” will show your current score and with “Ping” you can see if your game is running smoothly.

Hint: Press “Enter” to use chat.

Press and hold down “Tab” to view the current round‘s stats.

Some map architects install cannons which later can be used by gamers playing the map. Press and hold down the action key “E” to mount the cannon, press “Spacebar” to dismount.

Press and hold down “E” to mount a cannon, press “Spacebar” to dismount.

We have now entered a Deathmatch. Everyone on the map tries to defeat everyone else. “Kills” indicates how many people have already been defeated in this round, “Count” indicates the total number of defeats needed to end this round prematurely.

Play Deathmatch if you want the classic.

This mode is called CTF or Capture the Flag. Here, the goal for each team is to pick up the flag seen in this picture and carry it to the opponent’s flag spot. You can see the flag spots on the radar.

If a player carrying the flag is defeated, this player will re-spawn at a random spawn point on the map and drop the flag in the process. Once this happens, the flag shows up again on everyone’s radars and all players can pick it up again wherever the defeated former carrier dropped it.

The red and blue number on top of the screen indicate how many times one team has succeeded to drop the flag at the designated drop spot. Whoever succeeds most to do so in one round, wins.

The bar underneath indicates how far away you are from either of the drop spots.

Hint: The blue team will have to take the flag to the red drop sport and vice versa.

Pick up the flag and take it to your opponents’ drop spot.

The last mode we are introducing is called Defusion. Here, the red team’s goal is to plant a bomb and the blue team’s goal is to disarm it.

All members of team red carry a bomb (along with their weapons of course). It is their goal to reach one of the two Defusion spots which each Defusion map contains.

Plant and disarm the bomb by pressing and holding down the action key “E”. This will take some time so bring along team members for protection! Once a bomb has been planted on the blast spot, a countdown will start which is visible to everyone on the map. When time is up, the bomb goes off and the blue team loses. If the bomb does not go off in the specified time, team red will lose.

You can see both Defusion spots on your radar at all times.

Plant a bomb at one of the two Defusion spots and press and hold down the action key “E” to arm (red team) or disarm (blue team) it.

When the game is over, you will be given the opportunity to rate the map you just played. You have a stack of 50 pieces of so-called “Star Dust” per day to give out for rating maps. Rating is valuable for both you and the map creator (Master) as it converts Star Dust into Brick Points which will be added to both of your accounts right away.

Use your Star Dust to rate a map. You have 50 Star Dust to give out every day. Star Dust is converted into Brick Points for both you and the Map creator when given out.

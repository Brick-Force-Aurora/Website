 About Bricks

Anything you can place on the map in Build Mode is called a brick, even the objects that don’t look like bricks such as flowers, flags, ladders, etc. All bricks fall under three main categories: General Bricks, Colorful Bricks and Deco Bricks.

Anything you can place on the map in Build Mode is called a brick.

What are General Bricks?

These basic bricks include a wide variety of basic possibilities; concrete, wood and even green grass. With these bricks, you can build entire cities; the sky is the limit!

General Bricks are the standard in Brick-Force.

What are Colorful Bricks?

Colorful Bricks are a special type of brick. They differ from Deco Bricks as they only provide a certain special color brick such as pink or neon blue.

Colorful Bricks make your world varied.

What are Deco Bricks

Deco Bricks are a special type of brick. They differ from Colorful Bricks as they include interactive objects like ladders and turrets, and objects such as traffic cones or flashing lights.

Deco Bricks make your map even cooler.

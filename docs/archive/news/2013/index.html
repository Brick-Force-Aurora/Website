  • New Game Mode: FreeFall

    The most fun you can have online

    December 13, 2013

    FreeFall brings a whole new style of game-play to Brick-Force, as players compete against one another to avoid falling through the map. Equipped with a build gun and a “Fever Bar” that fills as you remove and add bricks to give you a burst of speed, your objective is to remove the ground from other players. This frantic new game mode features Power-Up Boxes which spawn around the map, unlocking tools to help you achieve your goal.

    There are several surprises to uncover in the Power-Up Boxes:

    • Pick-up bombs that drop on your enemy
    • Smoke grenades to obstruct their vision of map holes
    • Fever refills that will instantly boost your “Fever-Bar”
    • Wings that give you a limited time to fly
    • Mega-brickgun that will remove a 3 x 3 space
    • Power-up that instantly forces your enemy into the vortex under the map
       

    Challenge yourself with your parkour skills and enjoy the excitement of that well timed jump or brick removal, preventing your enemy from landing their jump.

    In physics, “freefall” is any motion of a body where its weight is the only force acting upon it. In Brick-Force, FreeFall is our latest bricktastic new game mode.

    The most fun you can have online? Play it today, and find out for yourself!

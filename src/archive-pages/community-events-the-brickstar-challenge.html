The Map Challenge

It’s time to dive into Build Mode and design some Space Themed creations. In the month of November, Infernum will only accept space-themed entries for Map of the Week. This means you must feature the new Booster Bricks, Skyboxes, or Adjustable Gravity Bricks for your map to be considered for selection.
To enter your map into the space race, simply take a screenshot and post it in the submissions thread. Remember to include your username and the registered map name in your posting.
Win a goodie package from the Infernum team, along with the usual Map of the Week rewards if your map is selected.

FAQ

What is a Premium Account? 

Premium status can be purchased in the shop for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. It offers you various benefits which gamers without Premium don't have.

How do I get a Premium Account? 

Purchase it in the shop [subcategory: 'Premium']. As soon as the purchase is finished, your account status will be switched to Premium and remain active for the duration of the package.

Which benefits do I have from upgrading my account to Premium? 

Premium status includes the following features: - The Heavy Brick-Gun for free
- An item which significantly shortens your respawning time
- An item which heals you automatically once per spawn
- 2 additional map slots
- A 10% Force Point Bonus
- A 10% XP Bonus
- All Brickstar bricks
As soon as the status package runs out or is terminated, all special features are deactivated.

What does a Premium account cost? 

As we offer different packages, prices differ as well. Right now, you have the following choices:
1 month (30 days) - $3.99
3 months (90 days) - $10.99 ($3.66 per month)
6 months (180 days) - $19.99 ($3.33 per month)
12 months (360 days) - $34.99 ($2.91 per month)

Is my Premium account bound to any conditions? 

The only condition is the duration itself. This depends on the package you bought. Right now, the duration period starts at the moment of your purchase. This can last 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. It is prolonged automatically when the package runs out, unless it is terminated by the user beforehand.

What happens to the items I buy during Premium status after this status ends? 

As soon as Premium status has ended, all special items you received for choosing Premium are deactivated. However, they remain existant in the background. If you want to use them again, you will have to get back to upgrading our account. All normal items which you might have purchased during this time are not affected by Premium status running out.

Do I need to terminate the subscription or does it run out on its own? 

The term of notice is 2 weeks for all packages. Depending on the payment method, if the termination is not done on time, the subscription will be automatically prolonged by the duration of the previously used package.

 How to create a map

Hi dearest player of Brick-Force! Let us get started by choosing a channel to play in:

  • Beginner is for brand new players who want to check out the game in a small tutorial first. Once you have reached the level of Corporal, you will no longer be able to enter the beginner channel.
  • Battle takes you directly into play mode. For the soldiers among you.
  • Map Edit will lead you right to the Build mode. For the born architect.
  • ClanMatch is for Clan fights only.

Double-click on any channel you want to join or click the “Play” symbol on the top left. The tabs sort all existing channels. They are meant to give you a better overview. Don’t worry, the decision you make here is not final. You can always change channels within the game.

Choose the beginners' channel if you need a game introduction, the editing channel to build maps or the battle channel to play against others. Channel selection is not final.

Here, you have the opportunity to either build a brand new map or to play against other people in the various play modes. Just press the “Create” button, and you will end up in the map creation interface if you have chosen a map editing channel or the play mode selection interface if you are in a battle channel.

Press “Create” to either create a new map or a room to play against others.

There are two different ways of building a map. If you have already built and saved a map in the past, you will find it in this interface. Here, you can either choose to modify an existing map or to create a brand new one.

“Title” refers to the title of the room. There will be a default line in here whenever you create maps. However, you can change this line by clicking on the “Title” bar. A room title is mandatory.

The little lock symbol on the top right of your game window enables you to encode your map with a password. Whenever somebody wants to even view your map, they will have to enter this password. Right now, a password can consist of 4 characters. Creating a password is optional.

With the “Max Players” option, you can define how many players you intend to allow to work with you on your map. The maximum number you can choose here is 8, including yourself.

In the map editing channel, the play modes Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch, CTF, Defusion and Tower Defense are not selectable. Go to the Shooter tutorial to learn more about these modes.

Choose an old map for editing or an empty field for an empty map and press “Approve”.

If you have chosen to create a new map, you will see this screen. Naturally, if you are playing with a brand new character, this screen will come up instantly. Naming your map is mandatory. Enter your map’s name in the “Map Title” bar.

Your new map will be completely empty, apart from two parameters which you can set right here: Sky and Landscape. The sky can be blue or orange, cloudy or clear, as you like it. The landscape defines the map size as well as the ground you are building on. ”F” describes the amount of floors your underground will have.

Hint: You will not be able to change these parameters once you have created and saved the map.

Name your map, choose a sky type and a type of underground tiling and press “OK”.

Now, the real fun begins! At the top of your screen, you see a selection of bricks you can choose from. This is your brick bar which is used as a short cut. In our example, pressing 3 will make dark green bricks come out of our brick gun. You can also scroll through brick types.

Underneath the brick bar, you can see the amount of bricks already used in your map. This includes the underground which is why this empty map contains of 2,500 bricks already. “General bricks” will show the amount of standard bricks, “Special bricks” will show the amount of Color or Deco bricks used.

We now have a multitude of tools and items which will make the life of a Brick builder easier. You can look forward to improved Brick-Guns in the sandbox mode. Also, behold the coolest of all skills: Flying with the new Jetpack! In addition, the “Swappie” and the “Streamliner” will make the realization of huge building projects a lot easier: Create a row of bricks with only a few clicks or replace all bricks of one type with another.

Use numbers or the mouse wheel to scroll through the brick bar, see if your special bricks are active and gain an overview over how many bricks you have already used.

Create a tree if you feel like it! Once you have chosen the type of brick you want to use for construction, aim your brick gun at the ground. The green crosshair will signal exactly where your brick will end up. You can also see how many meters distance there are between yourself and your target.

Deleting bricks is just as easy: aim your gun at the brick you want to remove and then press right-click.

The little map on the bottom right shows your map’s future thumbnail. A thumbnail is a small picture which represents a much bigger one. You can change your map’s thumbnail by positioning yourself somewhere else. See where your thumbnail shows up here.

Set a brick by aiming your brick gun’s crosshair at the point of your choosing and click your left mouse button. Delete a brick by aiming and right-clicking.

Pressing “Ctrl” will take you to the administration interface. Here, you can see the Master (owner) of a map. The Master is always the person who first created a map.

Only the owner can grant and deny the rights to make changes in a map. Just check the “Permissions” box underneath, and your friend can work together with you. If your map does not have a password, anyone who sees it in the main screen will be able to enter as well. Kick out people who you do not want viewing your map.

Press “Ctrl” to view the administration interface and choose who you want helping you build you map.

Of course, the ten bricks in the short cut bar are not everything we have to offer! Press “B” to view the entire arsenal. Choose from all “General bricks” right away. Click on “Color bricks” or “Deco bricks” to see all the other bricks you can build with.

Press “B” to open the brick selection interface.

Move any ten bricks you want into your short cut bar by clicking on them in the brick selection interface and placing them in the brick bar. Close the brick selection interface by pressing “B”.

Hint: Pointing your mouse at a certain type of brick and clicking your mouse wheel will move that brick type directly into your brick bar.

Use click and drop to place any bricks of your choosing in the short cut bar.

Of course, maps are most fun to build if a whole bunch of people have fun playing in them once they are finished. The most basic game mode required for a map that you and your friends can actually play in is called the “Team” mode. As each map can be played by a maximum of 16 people (for example eight red vs. eight blue in the Team mode), you will have to place 16 spawn points on each map, even if you plan to only ever play four vs. four in this map. Whenever somebody dies in-game, they will re-spawn at one of these points.

Place a total of 16 spawn points to make the map playable in at least one of the play modes.

When you find that your map is ready to be used by the rest of the world to play in, you need to first register it. Access the menu shown here by pressing “Esc”.

Do not forget to save your map every now and then. Exit the room if you no longer feel like building maps. Quit the game (if you have to). Or press Cancel to get back to the building mode.

Press “Esc” to view this interface. Save your map whenever you can and register it to make it publicly available.

Once you have pressed “Register”, you will see this information screen which tells you if your map is playable or not. The green remark “Map is ready to be registered.” is the ticket to registration. However, our tutorial map, right now, only fulfills the preconditions for Team mode. All other modes are grey and cannot be checked at this point.

Furthermore, this interface shows you how much your map has cost up to this point. It can also be accessed at any time during building by pressing and holding down the “Tab” key.

Choose “Register” to view this interface. Check if your map is playable in one or more modes and see how many General points it will cost to make it public.

Here, you can see the preconditions necessary for all available game modes in one single map.

The green flags are needed for “Deathmatch” mode. Find out more about this in the battle tutorial Deathmatch.

The three flags with the checkered foundation are required for the “Capture the Flag” (CTF) mode. The goal here is to snatch a flag and carry it to a designated point. Find out more about this in the battle tutorial CTF Mode.

The two Defusion spots you see in the back are filled with explosives but the bomb is missing. Find out more about this in the battle tutorial Defusion.

Check out the Shooter tutorial here to learn more about all play modes.

Our map has now been filled with the necessary bricks for all available gaming modes. Now, it is up to you to fill your map with people by registering it. Make it public by clicking “Register”. Let the battle begin!

Click “Register” to make your map public for everyone to play in it.

 Brick Brigade

The Community Program

Do you love building, sharing and playing? The Brick Brigade aims to reward those who want to share their Brick-Force experience, build the coolest maps and develop the community.

The Brick Brigade celebrates everything that’s great about Brick-Force. Whether you’re a Tremendous Tweeter or a Wiki Wordsmith, there’s a place for you in the Brick Brigade.

Have you completed the requirements for an exclusive forum title? Send an email to community@brickforce-aurora.de with the relevant information and links to claim your reward!

Brick-Agent

Become a member of the Brick-Brigade and volunteer to be a part of this community fan program.

Brick-Agents are volunteer members of the Brick-Brigade. They have shown their outstanding dedication to Brick-Force in being a part of the community. Agents use their resources and work with each other to better our community.

Community Representative

Community Representatives are highly active on the official forum and hand-picked by the Infernum Team.

You've come a long way, soldier! The highest possible rank in the Brick Brigade. Community Representatives are responsible for maintaining harmony on the forums and providing feedback to the dev team.

Community Moderator

Community Moderators are active on the official forum and assist in keeping the forums a positive environment.

If you are selected for this prestigious position, you will moderate the forums, help generate constructive discussion and empower the community.

Brilliant Builder

Create a Brick-Force map that achieves Top 3 in a seasonal map contest.

Your creativity is inspiring. Your planning is immaculate. You've made one of the best ever Brick-Force maps, as voted for by the community.

Brainy Builder

Creation of a Brick-Force map that is selected for 'Map of the Week'.

Your self initiative and cool ideas got you selected for "Map of the Week". Your map not only looks great but is fun to play. Well done, soldier!

Yummy YouTuber

Upload at least 10 Brick-Force videos on a YouTube channel with over 500 subscribers.

You built and blasted your way to victory and entertained the community along the way. Your YouTube videos are a source of inspiration to all of us.

Badass Blogger

Create a Brick-Force Tumblr blog that fulfills the requirements to be featured on the fansite page.

You grabbed your brick gun, loaded up your sniper, and blogged about your escapades! Your Tumblr blog is read by many looking for motivation on the battlefield.

Streaming Sensation

Record at least 10 hours of Brick-Force footage on TwitchTV.

In the heat of battle, you managed to remain calm and even stream live video updates from the trenches on TwitchTV. You should be proud, soldier!

Terrific Touter

Upload at least 10 Tout videos about Brick-Force on a Tout profile with at least 50 followers.

You’ve built and blasted your way to victory, now Tout it out! Your Tout videos will spread the word and rally the troops.

Wiki Wordsmith

Contribute to at least 15 articles on the official Brick-Force Wiki.

Blasting your way through the world of bricks, you've also found time to share your knowledge on the Brick-Force Wiki. Demonstrate your dedication and prove your expertise.

Tremendous Tweeter

Gather at least 100 Twitter followers and Tweet about Brick-Force at least 20 times!

Tweeting is fun, especially in the heat of battle. Tweet about Brick-Force and engage with the Official Brick-Force Twitter. You'll be well on your way to victory!

Furious Facebooker

Be a fan of the Official Brick-Force Facebook page for at least 3 months with at least 50 engagements (comments, shares) on the page.

Sociable soldiers are the best team mates. Tell us about your Brick adventures, share the Brick-Force journey with your friends, comment, like and support our mission.

Fabulous Fansiter

Create a Brick-Force fansite that fulfills the requirements to be featured on the fansite page.

You've taken it to the next level with a fansite that grabbed our attention. Your page has provided a beacon of brick-light, soldier.

 Tutorial: Item Shop & Buying Tokens

If you want to buy the latest gun or one of the many dashing outfits, you will need to visit the Item Shop. Here, you will find everything Brick-Force has to offer. Some items will improve your chances of dodging bullets, while others will increase your XP earnings. Is your Force Point budget low? You can always buy Tokens here.

Click on the Item Shop button to enter.

When you have entered the Item Shop, moving your mouse over one item will show you its stats and cost. You can see your riches and the item categories on the left. Item categories sort all available items for better overview. If you choose “Clothing” for example, you will see all Outfits listed here. Click “Get Tokens” if you haven’t got enough of those yet or choose an item and click “Purchase”.

View all items which are sorted by categories, mouse over an item to view its stats and double-click if you want to buy it or click “Purchase”.

For this tutorial, we have picked the Bronto 1000 which is one of the Heavy weapons. Stats and a small description text can be found here as well as the means to buy this weapon. You can buy any items you want by checking one of the currencies. Please note that not all items are purchasable with Force Points. Some are only available for Tokens. If you choose to buy an item that is not available for permanent use, the price will change depending on which duration you’d like. In addition, items can be bought to gift to friends. Also, this screen shows any discount you might have received. The more you use weapons from one category, the more experienced you will get and the cheaper it will become to buy them. In the next step, you have to confirm your purchase. You will now find your item in the “My Items” section where you can use and equip it.

Select which currency you want to spend and buy an item for yourself or to give to a friend.

But what happens if you don’t have enough Force Points left to buy the cool stuff that is available in the Item Shop? That’s easy: just buy it for Tokens! If you are in-game, click on the “Get Tokens” button while in the Item Shop. You will end up at this screen. To log in, use the same data you would to log into the game.

Log into the Brick-Force website in order to buy Tokens or click on the “Get Tokens” button in-game.

If you are buying Tokens for the first time, the website will ask you which server you play on. Tokens are server-bound so be sure that you are choosing the correct server!

Choose your server here. The choice you make here defines where the Tokens end up.

In the next step, you can choose which method you would like to use for payment. For our example, we have selected PayPal. Payment methods do not influence the amount of money you pay but there might be special offers sometimes where one specific payment method is offering a discount. At the top of this page, you are able to change the language, on the right hand side, you find help if you run into any problems.

Choose a payment method and double-click on it.

There are many different amounts of Tokens you can buy. The greater the amount of Tokens, the cheaper the price per Token is. Right now, the biggest package we are offering contains 36,000 Tokens. The next screens guide you through whichever method of payment you have chosen step by step.

Buy a small or big pack of Tokens. The bigger the pack, the smaller the price per Token.

  • Regarding the Future

    August 22, 2017

    Greetings Community,

     

    As you might have noticed, no developer-diary has been published. Fact is, sadly: we had to stop the development of Brick-Force. The original developer left us the game in a state that makes it impossible for us to extend or update the game. That means we are not able to apply bugfixes or new content.

     

    At the same time, we must announce that our last active member, WashburneX, will leave the game. Support and sales will no longer happen. We hope we will find a solution for the sales, but for the time being, there won’t be any new sales.

     

    If you have any support-requests, please ask the remaining community-moderators and your co-players. All request we receive that are not payment-related will not be handled.

     

    Regarding the elephant in the room: No, we will not shut down the game yet. We will inform you when the time comes.

     

    Thank you all for your loyalty and the fun we had. We hope we can continue to have more of it for a long time to come.

    Thanks for everything and till then!

    Cheers!

    WashburneX & Your Brick-Force-Team

  • [gamigo] The Golden Ticket

    June 23, 2017

    On August 23, 2017, the #gamigoVIParty starts! We have prepared a little surprise for this unprecedented event, just for you. We are giving away some prizes beforehand and the main prize is a round-trip-carefree package for you and a companion of your choice. This package includes a golden ticket, which not only guarantees you VIP entrance to the party, but also guarantees free drinks for the whole evening. Attention! - we put one more thing on it and not only take care of your round trip expenses, but your overnight hotel stay! In addition you have the possibility to visit the Gamescom for a whole day with this ticket. Does that sound good? Good!

    What do you have to do to win?
    Just send us a short application! Impress us with your creativity! There are hardly any limits.
    You can send us the application in the form of a video (whether you use your characters from the game or if you dare to go into the big wide world), a short story with a maximum of 1,000 words, a motivation letter or a small collage. If you do not like our ideas, then let something else come to you – be unique and convince us that you really, really ... really want to join this party!

    So ... once again the most important in points!

    What is there to win?
    • 1st place: A round-care package for 2
    • 2nd place: VIP entrance to the party for 2
    • 3rd place: Entrance to the party for 2

    What do I have to do?
    • Create a video (whether in the game or outside), or
    • Write a short story, or
    • Create a collage, or
    • Let yourself be inspired by something else so you will stick out of the crowd!

    Are there rules?
    • You must be at least 18 years old to win.
    • Submit your application by midnight latest on 04.07.2017 in this form!
    • The short story must contain a maximum of 1,000 words.
    • gamigo reserves the right to exclude persons from participation if there is a reasonable suspicion that they have violated one of these conditions. In such cases, the profit can also be subsequently recognized and a substitute winner be issued.
    • The profit is non-transferable and can not be paid in cash as an alternative. Gamigo reserves the right to replace any individual profit at any time with an equal or higher value profit. An issue of profits to minors is excluded.
    • If there are differences of opinion regarding the rules, the course or the results of the contest, gamigo's decision is final. Any legal process is excluded.
    • The data are treated confidentially by gamigo and in accordance with the legal data protection regulations.
    • gamigo Group employees and their members, as well as Gamemaster and their dependents are prohibited from entering this contest.


    We wish you good luck and we hope to see you at the gamigoVIParty!

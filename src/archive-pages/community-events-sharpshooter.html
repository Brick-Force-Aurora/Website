 Sharpshooter

Introduction

Shoot, dodge, and blast your way to the top! Are you skilled enough to be crowned a Sharpshooter? Only time can tell, as the Brick-Force community has just two weeks after the launch to prove themselves.

To grab yourself a sack full of Tokens, you’ll need to get your name on the Sharpshooter Leaderboard by defeating opponents and securing a high K/D ratio.

Grand Prizes*

After two weeks, the final Top 3 Sharpshooterswill win the following:

1st Place
- iPad3
- GamesCom Ticket
- Full set of permanent armor and weapons
- €20 / $25 of Tokens
2nd Place
- Full set of permanent armor and weapons
- €15 / $20 of Tokens
3rd Place
- Full set of permanent armor and weapons
- €10 / $15 of Tokens

What is a Kill-to-Death ratio?

Your K/D ratio is how many kills you have managed to score divided by how many times you have died. Overall, if you have more kills than deaths, you would have a positive K/D Ratio. We are also keeping track of in-game activity – meaning you must achieve a minimum level of activity to be included in the leaderboard.

Tokens for the Top 100

Not quite skilled enough to make the Top 3? No worries, there’s Tokens for everyone who makes it into the Top 100. Simply break into the leaderboard by 26 July to win a sack full of Brick-Force Tokens!

Ranking Token Reward
4 to 10 1,500 Tokens
11 to 20 1,000 Tokens
21 to 50 500 Tokens

76 to 100 100 Tokens

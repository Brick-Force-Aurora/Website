 Season Overview

Overview of the Brick-Force Seasons

Welcome to the overview page for the Brick-Force Seasons. Find detailed information on every major update on this page.

  • Brick-Force
  • Brickstar
  • Bricksaga
  • Cowboys & Bricks

Season 1: Brick-Force

The first season offers you everything a real Brick-Forcer needs: Heaps of brick that let you build anything a true architect requires as well as all kinds of items from A to Z for shooter fans. Brick-Force can be played through a client or in your browser, the latter includes Macs.

Screenshots

Features

Build Mode

Decorative bricks, colorful bricks, standard bricks and interactive elements like turrets make each map individual and new. Combination possibilities are nearly endless and you will receive a reward for your building efforts: Define a download fee for your maps and receive points when they get rated by others.

Play Modes

Experience a truly awesome selection from more than 300 items. Weapons, accessories, outfits and consumables will prep you for war. Combat is placed in both, classic match situations like Team and Solo Deathmatch and CTF or in newer concepts such as Build & Destroy and Defense.

Bricks

Standard bricks are the simplest bricks available to you and thus the ones which are probably used most: Concrete, Grass or Wood- there are loads of different basic desings. Colorful bricks make your map more diversified and Decorative ones breathe life into your world.

Daily Missions

Complete them to receive more Force Points and the sought-after Coins. Even more reason to prove your skillz on the battlefield.

Pick 'N' Win

A raffle without any blanks. You will need Coins or Tokens to take part here. Whichever star card you turn over- you will always win something. And if you're extra lucky, you might even win one of our special prizes.

Upgrades

Weapons and accessories can be enhanced. Everything you need are the appropriate Upgrade Gems. This way, you don't only receive stronger weapons but also armor which provides you with better protection on the battlefield.

Season 2: Brickstar

Brick-Force's Season Two update of intergalactic proportions! Space is no longer the limit - travel beyond the stars with interstellar outfits, weapons and bricks. Aliens, Monsters, Spacemen and Heroes are waiting for you. So, which side will you take?

Screenshots

Features

Functional Bricks

In addition to decorative bricks for spaceships, satellites and planets, we've got interactive bricks now: toxic barrels which explode when shot at and boosters which catapult you right across the map are just a couple of examples of what you can enjoy in Season Two.

Adjustable Gravity

Map gravity can be adjusted by placing gravity bricks. Map builders can choose whether players will weightlessly float across the map or get into trouble by being slowed down. Gravity bricks come in two flavors, destructible and indestructible - this way, gravity can be adjusted during a match, creating new tactical challenges.

Spacy Weapons

There's new weapons to suit all tastes awaiting for you. Some of the best space armaments feature special functions such as rocket launchers, burst fire and fire grenades.

Space Gear

A range of outfits for every kind of creature you might run into up in space: aliens, spacemen, flying heroes and monsters. You'll be spoilt for choice when it comes to character customization. Our advice: mix it all together so you become the alienmonsterheroman. Or woman.

Starry Skies

A heap of new skyboxes will make your spaceships and strange planets way more believable. Get lost in the stars with beautiful black skies, starry wonders or a burning-red firmament.

Season 3: Bricksaga

Knights, castles, adventure! Brick-Force gets medieval with Bricksaga, bringing you epic new content: build towering fortresses with deep torch-lit dungeons full of traps. Battle with swords, crossbows, and magic wands in magnificent castles. Experience a new fantastic Brick-Force world and take an active part in shaping it!

Screenshots

Features

Portals and Pitfalls

Become an architect: Let your creativity run wild with the new deco bricks. Build majestic cathedrals with colorful windows or gloomy fortresses with torches, banners, and decorative suits of armor. You can also include nasty traps and give your map new strategic possibilities using portals.

A Hard-Hitting Arsenal

You don’t think a knight armed with a sword and crossbow has a chance against a Brick-Soldier? Then you haven’t tried out the new Season 3 weapons yet! In addition to a variety of crossbows, you can also take out opponents in close combat with a selection of medieval melee weapons. The poison bombs create a cloud of noxious smoke that can effectively block your enemy’s path.

Legendary Armor

Haven’t you always wanted to be a part of a thrilling adventure? Save a damsel in distress perhaps? Maybe storm the gates of an enemy castle as a valiant knight? Or do you prefer to sneak around like a thief in the night? Are you a sharpshooting archer, picking off enemies from afar? Maybe it’s time to reveal your hidden potential as a mage and shoot fireballs from your wand? Bricksaga offers all this and much more.

New Gameplay

Your level makes a real difference: As you progress in the game, you will be able to use more and better equipment. You also get cool rewards for every level-up.

More Fairness

The new channels promote a balanced gaming experience. You can play in a channel where you’ll only find other players in the same level bracket as you, and you’ll even get bonuses in XP and Force Points for playing there. The Fun Channel on the other hand is a free-for-all. There you can meet up with friends and enemies of all levels to explore the wide range of unique maps in Brick-Force.

Season 4: Cowboys & Bricks

Howdy, partners! Ever dreamed of dueling at high noon? Being a cool gunslinger, or even creating your own Wild West out of bricks? Season 4: Cowboys & Bricks makes all those dreams come true! Battle like a true outlaw, hijack a train, be the most wanted player, and much more. Remember to keep your guns ready, soldiers, the game has just begun.

Screenshots

Features

Double the Fun

Feel the power of dual wielding guns, dynamite, pistols, repeating rifles, and other western classics. Just the armaments you need to survive in the Wild West. Choose wisely and rodeo your way through the unpredictable threats ahead!

More Bricks!

Build your own western-themed world with new bricks like classy saloon doors, bursting cacti, explosive TNT barrels, and tall grass. That’s not enough? Now try to create your own train transport system. Nothing can stop you!

More Action

What could be more fun than crouching and picking up weapons in battle? We’ve got it all in Season 4! Duck and cover from your enemies when the battle goes wild with the crouch feature, then let them feel the burn of their own weapon after you pick it up. Plan your strategy well and bring the enemy team down!

True Western Outfits

Be the good-hearted Sheriff, protecting the Brick-Town from bandits. Be the Native American, always up for a challenge and eager to explore. Be the Mexican Muchacho, a mysteriously powerful and skilled gunslinger, or be the Bandit, break the rules, and steal some cash. Be what you want to be in Brick-Force, just don’t forget to watch your back! Bullets are flyin’!

New Game Mode

Wanted Mode: In these matches, one player or one from each team is “Wanted”. Kill them for extra scores and become the new most wanted. But watch out: The wanted criminal has powerful buffs granting them increased health points and attack power. Good luck, Brick-Cowboys!
